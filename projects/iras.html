The project aimed at analysing the rotational speed of different rings from a video of rotating rings densely packed in a box.

Implemented programs to apply image transformations, used cross-correlation method and calculated rotational velocity for all 180 rings at all time instances and performed data analysis.

Superimposed velocity vectors on original frames to visually compare ring velocities with each other.
